We're a team of trekkers, priests' sons, cooks and mountaineers from Uttarakhand, sharing the Himalayas the way we grew up with them.

Our story

From one trail
to sixty.

It started with a group of eight friends on Kedarkantha in the winter of 2012. Today we run more than 60 routes across Garhwal, Kumaon and Himachal, but every batch still gets the same thing: a leader who knows the mountain by heart, hot food at every camp and a safety plan for every turn.

For pilgrims, we bring the same care to the Char Dham. Hotels close to the temples, darshan help and a pace that suits every generation of the family.
